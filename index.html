Y2K FASHION

grool, obviously.

Butterfly clips. Rhinestone everything. Jeans that started well below the belly button. This was the look the whole world copied off — twice.

note passed in 3rd period Y2K fashion was so iconic that twenty-plus years later, people are still trying to make it happen again — and it's still fetch.

The Lookbook

Four outfit formulas the whole era ran on, flat-laid straight off the bedroom floor.

The Uniform

Every plastic had one. The wardrobe rules of the era, ranked by how much glitter they involved.

ITEM 01

Low-Rise Jeans

Sat dangerously below the hip bones and somehow never came with a belt. Bonus points for a chain wallet.

ITEM 02

Butterfly Clips

A dozen of them, clipped into two front pieces, non-negotiable. Sparkly ones were reserved for special occasions.

ITEM 03

Velour Tracksuit

Matching set, rhinestone logo across the back, zero intention of ever going to the gym in it.

ITEM 04

Tiny Sunglasses

Barely covered the eyes, tinted pink or baby blue, worn indoors for no reason whatsoever.

ITEM 05

Baby Tee

One size too small on purpose, with a slogan that said the quiet part loud across the chest.

ITEM 06

Platform Flip-Flops

Beaded straps, a two-inch sole, and absolutely no arch support. Worn to the mall, the beach, everywhere.

ITEM 07

Chunky Highlights

Bleached in thick streaks and finished off with a crimping iron for that fresh-off-the-runway static look.

ITEM 08

Micro Handbag

Technically a purse, practically a coin pouch. Fit a flip phone and not one single other thing.
